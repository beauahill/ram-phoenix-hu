## The finding that changes the plan

Device 14 is **not a raw camera**. It is the composited **CVBS output of the 360 ECU**.
The ECU burns its own OSD into the video: view-select buttons (rear / overhead+rear / truck-bed),
zoom, close X, "Adjust Centerline", and its dashed centerline. Those are pixels, not Android views.

1. **Resolution ceiling is the ECU analog output (~720x480 class).** No Android-side change makes it sharper.
   Our app fixes lag, black-flash and layout, not source resolution.
2. **View switching (front / rear / 360) is a command to the ECU**, not a different Camera2 ID.
   Stock com.syu.us forwards touches on the OSD buttons to the ECU via canbus. Next RE target:
   that touch/command write path.
3. Our guidelines duplicate the ECU's own. Hide ours, or find the ECU command that disables its OSD.
4. The popup menu over the feed was com.syu.fytgesture (assistive touch), not the camera.
